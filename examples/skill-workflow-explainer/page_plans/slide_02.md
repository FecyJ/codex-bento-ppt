# slide_02 - 核心思想：把 PPT 当作专家团队交付物

## Page Role

Methodology.

## Core Message

质量来自角色拆分和阶段产物，而不是一次性生成。

## Content Blocks

1. Anti-pattern: topic in, template out.
2. Expert team model: consultant, researcher, architect, planner, designer, reviewer.
3. Outcome: every phase produces an artifact that can be inspected.

## Designer Handoff Draft

- Plain wireframe intent: left warning card, right role matrix, bottom result strip.
- Exact element placement: warning card 40% width; role grid 60% width.
- What must be emphasized: no one-click template.
- What may be omitted if crowded: role descriptions beyond 3 words.
- What should stay visually quiet: decorative lines.
